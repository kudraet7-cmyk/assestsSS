一、错题精讲共 2 题 · 含干扰项分析

第 30 题词汇 · 固定搭配班级错误率 41%
It helped us connect ____ each other.
你选 B. to ✗|正确 A. with ✓

错因 · 介词搭配混淆。

connect with sb ＝ 与某人建立联系、交往，强调人与人之间的双向关系。同族记忆：communicate with、get on with、share sth with sb、compare A with B。

你选的 to 为什么错：connect A to B 指物理连接——connect the printer to the computer。人不是设备，所以这里必须用 with。记一句话：接线用 to，交往用 with。

第 62 题阅读 · 短语辨析班级错误率 36%
Ray ____ his hand and the teacher let him answer.
你选 C. put on ✗|正确 A. put up ✓

错因 · 动词短语未成组掌握。

put up one's hand ＝ 举手（＝ raise one's hand）。up 表「向上」，手往上抬就是举手。语境也给了提示：后半句 the teacher let him answer（老师让他回答），只可能由「举手」引出。

put 家族一次记全：put on 穿上／上演 · put off 推迟 · put out 扑灭 · put away 收好 · put down 放下／写下。建议把这 5 个短语抄成一张卡片，每个配一个例句。

B. 阅读理解 · 细节定位 / 主旨 / 短语辨析 本卷该考点 4 题，你错 1 题

先读题干划出关键词，再回原文定位；主旨题看首尾句约 105 词 · 8 分钟
Lin Xiao is a Grade 6 student in Shanghai. Last term her class started a “Green Corner” in the school library. Every Friday afternoon the students bring old books and swap them with each other. “Now I can read twenty more books a year, and it costs nothing,” Lin Xiao says.
At first only eight students joined in. Their teacher, Mr Zhou, put up a poster near the school gate, and soon more than sixty students came. Some parents worried that the old books were dirty, so the students clean every book before they put it on the shelf. “The Green Corner does not only save money,” Mr Zhou says. “It connects children with each other, and it teaches them to care for things.”
  1. Where did the students start the “Green Corner”? （细节定位）
  2. Why did more students join later? （细节定位 · 找因果）
  3. What does the underlined word “swap” mean? A. buy　B. exchange　C. borrow　D. sell
  4. The best title for the passage is ____. A. A Busy Friday Afternoon　B. Mr Zhou's Poster　C. Books That Connect Us　D. How to Clean Old Books
参考答案 · 基础巩固：1. to（设备连接）　2. with（人际联系）　3. put up　4. put off　5. Put on　6. put out
同类变式：7. join（加入组织用 join，加入活动用 join in）　8. as（the same as）　9. so（前因后果）　10. but（转折）　11. 参考：Playing basketball connects me with new friends.
中考衔接：12. B　13. C　14. A　15. B　（14 题：空后是 with，四个选项中只有 connect 能与 with 构成「与……建立联系」，正是第 30 题的同一考点。）
短文精练：16. In the school library.　17. Because Mr Zhou put up a poster near the school gate.　18. B　19. C　（19 题：首段点出交换图书，末段 Mr Zhou 说 “It connects children with each other”，首尾呼应即主旨。）
